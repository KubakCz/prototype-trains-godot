# The suites

What each suite under `tests/` covers. How to run and write them is in [README.md](README.md).
Add a row when you add a suite.

| suite | what it covers |
| --- | --- |
| `turnout_geometry_test.gd` | branches welded to the points, tangent departure, which hand a turnout is, configuration warnings |
| `turnout_passage_test.gd` | the passage table straight through `Turnout.traverse`, every leg in both positions |
| `turnout_driving_test.gd` | a train driving all of it: diverging, being held and released, the dead-end siding, a body spanning two rails, points thrown underneath derailing it, points thrown at a held train letting it through |
| `main_scene_test.gd` | `scenes/main.tscn`, the playground: every branch welded, no configuration warnings, one of every junction kind, each train held at its red signal, every car of every train on the track, every way into every junction protected by a signal, the balloon and the wye turning a train round, the crossover, scissors, double slip and three-way routing as set, the freight line crossing the oval, the ground flat under the track but for the tail's one bridge and one tunnel |
| `trackside_posts_test.gd` | signal masts and turnout posts standing upright and reaching the ground on analytic hills, built in the test because every level now lies on a carved bed |
| `desert_level_test.gd` | `scenes/levels/desert.tscn`: every branch welded, no configuration warnings, the carved ground never rising into a passing train outside a tunnel mouth, the one bridge, the ridge line crossing 8 m over the tunnel line, each arrival held at its entry signal, the balloon loop turning a train round, the wye joining each leg to both others, the lake scissors crossing both ways |
| `ground_test.gd` | `Ground.surface_height_at` agreeing with a ray cast onto the mesh, on analytic hills built in the test and on both baked heightmaps |
| `turnout_click_test.gd` | throwing points by clicking the 3D marker and the floating widget, and the marker reporting the pointer over it, through the real input path (needs `-Windowed`) |
| `signal_passage_test.gd` | the stopping rule through `TrackWalker`: which direction a signal governs, the walks that ignore signals, a signal the nose has passed, one standing on a turnout, which side the mast lands on, configuration warnings |
| `signal_driving_test.gd` | trains at signals: held at danger and away when it clears, untouched by the signal facing the other way, not stranded when one goes back to danger underneath them, held past the points and on the branch |
| `signal_overlay_test.gd` | `MarkerOverlay`: signals and turnouts in one overlay, widgets shifted out of each other's way, all three collapse rules, hover reopening a collapsed widget, the direction a widget shows, the four turnout captions |
| `signal_click_test.gd` | changing a signal by clicking the mast and the floating widget, the mast reporting the pointer over it, and `player_operable = false` refusing a click (needs `-Windowed`) |
| `compound_turnout_test.gd` | `CompoundTurnout`: every kind's positions and cycle order, members taken off the player, a member thrown directly leaving it `MIXED`, the configuration warnings, one widget per assembly and none for members in every overlay mode, the assembly widget following the turnout caption, a double slip's two positions |
| `compound_passage_test.gd` | trains through the assemblies: a three-way in all three positions both ways, a crossover crossing one direction and holding the other two, a double crossover crossing all four, a double slip opening both straight routes or both slip roads, trains driving over, taking the slip and being held, the drawn routes matching the walks |
| `crossing_test.gd` | `Crossing`: located where the rails meet, attached to both rails, walked straight through, which trains are over it (on either rail, and a train crossing the scissors), the warnings and Locate Crossing fixing them |
| `compound_click_test.gd` | an assembly worked through the real input path: a click on one member's marker cycles all of it, a click on the widget cycles it exactly once, hovering a member counts as the assembly (needs `-Windowed`) |
| `debug_panel_test.gd` | the debug HUD: every section folded on open, the number keys, a click on a header, the cycle rows that carry the open presentation questions, the rail-lines row, and the `>` that follows the mouse (the last needs `-Windowed`) |
| `train_consist_test.gd` | a train as its cars end to end, the rates coming from the consist (load slows starting and stopping, length only starting), a wagon coupled on behind, every car following the locomotive across a crossover, the bogies staying on the rails with their wheels on the railheads, and a wreck keeping its bogies under it |
| `train_braking_test.gd` | getting going at the consist's rate, the service curve into a red signal, a signal put back late stopped for harder, one put back too late overrun and the train let go - standing or still braking, the player's stop, reversing at speed, the front marker's colours |
| `derailment_test.gd` | points thrown under a train, points set against it and the end of the line found too late, two trains touching head on and where tracks converge, trains abreast on parallel tracks not touching, a wreck staying put |
| `train_selection_test.gd` | the selected train drawn lighter blue, the train panel's keys acting on it - stop, go, let go, the consist keys, turning round only when standing - and doing nothing to a wreck |
| `train_click_test.gd` | selecting a train by clicking a car, a click on the ground clearing it, a click on a signal leaving it (needs `-Windowed`) |
| `station_test.gd` | a train stopping with its nose on the end of the platform it is heading for (the stop marks, clamped to the rail), either way on the same platform, on any of the station's tracks and across points onto a rail it is not on yet, through a signal at clear, held by one at danger before it instead, held by a red on the platform and arriving only once let up to the platform's end, waiting at the red exit signal after its stop, passing a station that is not next or that it is too fast to stop at, dwelling for the stop's time, a mine filling and a factory emptying the wagons in steps, coaches untouched, a wreck not dwelling |
| `spawner_test.gd` | trains at load and then only at connections, the minimum interval and the maximum count (a wreck counts), a taken track refused, out and in tracks, a route that matches its entry and type, the same seed the same trains; where a train is put - nose on the stop mark for the way it faces at a station, facing its stops, nose on the mark and the whole train outside at a gate, refused when the rail outside the mark is too short, and the spawner's warning for such a gate; the sector a new train needs empty - from 15 m behind its tail to the first signal ahead whatever it shows - and a new train rolling in to stand at its red entry signal; leaving by the destination once the tail clears the mark (complete or not), a non-destination connection and an in track as dead ends, a train held at the end of its destination leaving |
| `interest_point_test.gd` | the poles over the middle of the tracks and no shape or label of the point's own in 3D, one-word names, colours spread over the wheel and a custom colour taking the others' room, the badge on the marker layer - one per point, on top of the poles, the name inside it and its width following the name, a bare circle or square without names, never collapsed under any rule, letting clicks through - configuration warnings, a connection's outside, the room there and the spawn pose with the nose on the mark |
| `livery_test.gd` | the palette with no two shades of one colour, every stripe standing out from its body, random liveries, only the locomotive painted, selection lightening it and a wreck going grey |
| `route_display_test.gd` | the row of icons over a train - its stops in order and their colours, circle and square textures, flat billboards of fixed size with no text, over the locomotive rather than the middle of the train, the next stop bigger and at the nose end on screen, the same size whatever the camera's distance and twice it at twice the UI scale, dropping stops as they are reached, hidden for a wreck or in list mode - the list in arrival order, the panel rows that switch the display and the names, the interest panel and the route line of the train panel |
| `track_mesh_test.gd` | the generated track on four levels: built as each loads and in chunks, no two tracks' rails within a flangeway of each other anywhere, two blades and a frog per turnout, a diamond cut at all four corners, the blades swinging with the points, sleepers never crossing and long timbers at every turnout, buffer stops at dead ends but not in tunnels, the ballast reaching the ground, what a rebuild depends on |
| `structures_test.gd` | `scenes/demos/structures.tscn`: three bridges and a tunnel per line in its own style, long spans split into arches, trusses with piers, a curved truss split until the track stays inside it, the tallest and widest stock fitting through, the ground cut open behind each tunnel mouth, every train running over its bridges and through its tunnel; and the playground's and the desert's structures built and cleanly configured |
| `station_building_test.gd` | a station's building: a box with a gable roof in the station's colour (roof darker, repainted with the colour), beside the outermost track on the side asked for, the gap and size it is meant to have, standing on the ground, none for a gate or with no side; its platforms - an island where two tracks have room between them, an outer one where a track has none, none for a track hemmed in, each as long as its track's platform and rebuilt with it, low, in a lighter shade of the station's colour; exit signals found whatever they show and not past the points, a signal applying to its direction whatever it shows, the warnings for a station track without an exit signal (but not towards a buffer), an exit signal standing on the platform, and a gate track without an entry signal, coming and going |
| `stations_demo_test.gd` | `scenes/demos/stations.tscn`: every signal at danger at start, every way back from every junction meeting an entry or exit signal, no configuration warnings, an exit signal each way off every station track that is not a dead end and clear of the platform's end, room outside every gate track's mark for the longest train and an entry signal inside it |
